## General Provisions

**Tartarus Project** is an event-based Space Station 13 server created to host a variety of gameplay scenarios and events.

Unlike standard SS13 servers, there is no single fixed format of gameplay. One round may focus on serious RP, another may revolve around a scripted scenario, while a third may function as a free-form sandbox.

Before an event begins, players are informed of its specific rules, RP level, and any additional restrictions.

> **Important:** The rules of the current event take priority over the general server rules.

## 01 — Don't Be a Dick

Tartarus Project is not a standard SS13 server.

Rounds may differ significantly from conventional gameplay. Do not automatically assume how a station should operate or how a character should behave if the current event establishes different conditions.

Follow the current scenario and the instructions of the administration.

## 02 — Don't Be a Dick

This is the fundamental principle of the server.

There is a difference between a player who makes an honest mistake and a player who deliberately ruins the experience for everyone around them.

Mistakes are part of the game. Deliberately creating a miserable experience for others is not.

Try to be the former.

Ideally, neither.

## 03 — OOC and Metagaming

OOC interaction and the use of information obtained outside the game are prohibited unless the current round or event explicitly allows a low or nonexistent level of RP.

Information known to the player is not necessarily known to their character.

Do not use player knowledge to justify character actions without appropriate in-game reasoning.

## 04 — It's a Sandbox

Tartarus Project supports a variety of gameplay formats.

Depending on the event, players may be assigned a specific role, given the freedom to create their own character, or subjected to other participation requirements.

The conditions of a particular round are specified in the event announcement or communicated to players immediately before the game begins.

The degree of freedom may vary significantly from one event to another.

## 05 — ERP Is Prohibited

ERP content is strictly prohibited on the server regardless of circumstances, RP level, or the rules of the current event.

This restriction applies to gameplay, character interactions, and related content created within the server.

Specialized platforms exist for such formats.

## 06 — Event Rules Take Priority

Each event may establish its own rules, restrictions, and gameplay conditions.

If the rules of the current event differ from the general Tartarus Project rules, the event rules take precedence.

However, basic server restrictions are not automatically lifted simply because an event allows for a more unrestricted style of gameplay.

Unless the administration explicitly states otherwise, the general server rules remain in effect.

## 07 — No RDM

Do not kill or attack other players without appropriate in-game justification unless the current round or mode is explicitly built around widespread violence.

If the scenario involves war, a massacre, or another format where large numbers of deaths are expected, such actions may be part of the gameplay.

Simply wanting to have fun is not sufficient justification for pointlessly killing another player in a round where such behavior is not supported by the scenario.

## 08 — RP Level May Vary

The level of roleplay is determined by the current round, event, or mode.

It may range from serious RP to virtually no roleplay restrictions at all.

## 09 — Losing Is Part of the Game

Your character may lose, suffer serious consequences, find themselves in an impossible situation, or die.

This is part of the gameplay.

The administration is not obligated to save your character from the consequences of their actions simply because the situation did not unfold as the player wanted.

A character's victory is not a requirement for enjoying a round.

Sometimes a good story ends with your character losing.

## 10 — Have Fun

The primary goal of Tartarus Project is to create interesting situations and host events that people want to participate in.

Enjoy the game, but do not take that opportunity away from everyone else.

If an action makes the game significantly worse for those around you without a reasonable in-game justification, you probably should not do it.

## Rule Priority

In the event of a conflict between rules, the following order applies:

The rules of a specific event define the conditions of the current gameplay.

The general rules apply to all situations that have not been modified or clarified by the event rules.

If a situation is not directly covered by the rules, the administration makes the final decision based on their overall purpose: maintaining an engaging and acceptable gameplay experience for everyone involved.

## Conclusion

The rules are not intended to cover every possible situation.

They establish the basic principles within which the administration and players are expected to interact.

If you are unsure whether an action is acceptable, ask yourself a simple question:

> **"Does this create an interesting gameplay situation, or am I simply making the game worse for everyone else?"**

In most cases, the answer will be obvious.

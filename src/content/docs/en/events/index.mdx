## General Information

This section of the archive contains records and information on the domains in which simulations may be conducted.

Each section of this part of the archive contains comprehensive information about the corresponding domain, including its contents, environmental characteristics, and the intended simulation scenario.

The following is a list of active domains currently available for loading:

- **Trainstation (The Final Trainstation)** — a planetary domain representing a narrative simulation centered around the transportation of a designated cargo. The events take place on an unnamed planet, where test subjects are given control of active rolling stock modified for long-distance travel. Further information on this domain can be found in the [corresponding section](trainstation).

## Morphological Engine

The **Morphological Engine** is the primary system protecting the internal space of **Belus-06** from the spread of the Khara mutation. The device is installed in the engineering car and serves as the sole source of the protective field for the entire train.

The engine can operate in three modes simultaneously. Each mode serves a separate purpose and requires additional power. The modes can be combined.

The modes operate independently and can be enabled simultaneously. For example, combining **Barrier + Containment** allows the crew to prevent mutants from physically entering the protected area while simultaneously slowing existing infections.

> **Important:** Power consumption from each mode is cumulative. With Barrier, Containment, and Isolation enabled, the engine consumes **160 KW** of additional power.

### Protected Area

The protective effects do not cover the entire world. They only apply to predefined areas of the train. In the standard configuration, the engine protects the train's internal compartments.

When the engine is activated, the corresponding areas acquire protective properties. This allows Khara systems to identify the compartment as an area covered by the Morphological Engine.

Barrier operates directly along the boundary of such an area. Therefore, a mutant outside the protected area cannot enter the compartment, while creatures already inside can move freely and leave the protected area.

### Engine Controls

To turn the engine on or off, interact directly with the device.

The operation takes **10 seconds** and can be interrupted. After each activation or deactivation, the engine must also undergo a **60-second** cooldown period.

When activated, the engine checks the available power supply. If there is insufficient power for the selected combination of modes, the device automatically shuts down.

After a successful startup, all living creatures within the protected area briefly experience the effects of the field. A visual effect and a system message indicating engine operation will also appear.

If the available power becomes insufficient while the engine is operating, it will automatically shut down.

After confirmation, a mandatory calibration procedure begins. It consists of three consecutive operations:

**Screwdriver → Wrench → Welding Tool.**

Each calibration stage has a chance of failure. If an error occurs, calibration is interrupted and the engine begins showing signs of overload.

After all three stages are completed successfully, the selected modes become the engine's active configuration.

> **Important:** Changes to the selected modes are not applied immediately. The complete configuration and calibration cycle must be completed first.

### Operational Recommendations

**Barrier** is most useful for physically containing infected entities and restricting access to protected compartments.

**Containment** is intended for keeping an infection under control over an extended period and is particularly useful in areas where infected individuals are already present.

**Isolation** should be reserved for critical situations. Despite its high effectiveness against Khara, this mode significantly increases power consumption and may harm infected crew members.

In practice, the selected configuration should be based on the available power supply and the current state of the infection.

> **Warning:** An open control panel prevents the engine from being activated. Complete all configuration and calibration procedures before operating the engine.

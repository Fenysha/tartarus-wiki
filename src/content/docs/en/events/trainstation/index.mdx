## Domain Description

**The Final Trainstation** is a self-contained narrative domain centered around a prolonged railway journey across an infected planet.

The simulation takes place on an unnamed planet, a significant portion of whose surface has been contaminated by the **Khara** pathogen.

The test subjects form the crew of the experimental railway train **Belus-06** — a modified train equipped with the systems required for long-term autonomous expeditions. The train serves simultaneously as a means of transportation, a mobile base, and the crew's primary survival tool.

Unlike most known domains, the geographical structure of **The Final Trainstation** is not fixed. Routes, station locations, and individual sections of the surrounding environment may change between simulation runs. This provides considerable variation when the domain is reloaded.

The crew's primary objective is to progress along the route and complete the assigned transportation task. However, the length of time spent in individual areas is directly linked to the increasing level of threat.

## Environmental Conditions

At the beginning of the simulation, the infection already covers a significant portion of the planet. Starting stations generally remain relatively safe and may be used by the crew to replenish supplies, repair the train, and prepare for further travel.

As the crew moves farther from the initial zone, the influence of the **Khara** pathogen becomes increasingly pronounced. Environmental contamination rises, along with the likelihood of encountering hostile lifeforms and other hazards.

The crew is advised to treat every stop as temporary. Prolonged stays in a single location significantly increase the likelihood of additional threats emerging.

## Simulation Features

The defining feature of **The Final Trainstation** is constant movement. Unlike most domains, where the crew can relatively freely determine the pace of their progress, stopping itself constitutes a risk here.

The **Belus-06** must remain operational, as its condition directly determines the crew's ability to leave infected areas and continue the mission.

Damage to the train, resource shortages, or prolonged stays at individual stations can turn a relatively safe route into a critical situation.

Additionally, a number of standard systems present in the base simulation configuration have been modified or completely disabled to accommodate the conditions of this domain.

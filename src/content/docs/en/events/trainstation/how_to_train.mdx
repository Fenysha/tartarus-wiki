## Train Structure

This guide contains instructions for operating **Belus-06**, maintaining its functionality, and expanding the train.

Before proceeding with the maintenance of individual systems, it is important to understand which areas are considered part of the train, where construction is permitted, and what happens if a crew member leaves the train while it is moving.

## Loss of Crew Members

During the operation of **Belus-06**, crew members may find themselves outside the train's designated area.

Anyone remaining outside the train's operational area when movement begins is considered lost. Returning to the train after departure is impossible.

It is also strongly advised not to remain directly beneath walls, overhead structures, or other protruding parts of the train while it is moving. Due to the simulation's collision mechanics, contact with a moving structure may result in immediate death.

> **Important:** The inability to survive or return to the train after its departure is a limitation of the domain simulation, not an inherent property of the **Belus-06** design.

## Train Movement

**Belus-06** requires a continuous supply of power to move. This power must be supplied directly to the train engine.

<ImagePanel
  src="trainstation/train_engine.png"
  alt="Belus-06 engine"
  caption="Belus-06 engine"
  reference="IMG-0001"
  source="ARCHIVE/TRAINSTATION"
/>

The engine is located in the engineering car and passively consumes approximately **60 MW** of power while the train is moving.

If power is insufficient or the engine is damaged, the train will automatically stop. The train can also be stopped manually using the control console located in the headquarters car. Only one such console exists on the train.

> **Important:** Movement terminals are distributed throughout different sections of the train. They can be used to obtain information about the current location of **Belus-06**.

## Morphological Engine

The **Morphological Engine** is the primary system protecting the internal space of **Belus-06** from the spread of the Khara mutation. The device is installed in the engineering car and serves as the sole source of the protective field covering the entire train.

For more information, see the [corresponding article](../morph_engine).
